The Davanagere Tradition

Every recipe adheres strictly to unhurried, time-tested preparation without compromise or shortcuts.

I

Fresh Churned Benne

II

Wood-Fired Cast Iron

III

Natural Stone Ferment

Heirloom grains ground on natural granite stone rollers and fermented naturally for 14 hours. Zero artificial soda or rising agents.

Born in Heritage

The Royal Art of Benne

In the historic kitchens of Davanagere, making dosa was an art form reserved for celebrations. The secret lay in the unhurried preparation: local raw rice, whole white urad, and the addition of freshly churned butter at precisely the right temperature.

Maharaja Benne Dosa brings this authentic culinary standard to Latur. We don't rush our batters, we don't dilute our chutneys, and we treat every morning breakfast as an aristocratic feast.
